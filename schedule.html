My schedule

Your homework, week by week. Everything is due by Wednesday at 11:59pm. Anything not done by then means a detention on Friday after school.

How it works

  • 6, 9 and 15-mark questions: click Start and the question opens in the right marking tool with its case study. Type your answer or upload photos of a handwritten one, then get it marked.
  • Calculations: work each one out, then choose what it means for the business.
  • Data drill: the case study opens on screen. You choose answers by clicking and only type the numbers you work out.
  • Knowledge quiz: done on the site. Your first answer is the one that counts.
  • Everything is ticked off here automatically once it is done or marked.
