Assessment
Three papers, same structure.
Every paper has two case studies. Each case study is followed by five compulsory questions worth 45 marks.

Paper 1

Unit 3.1 — Marketing and finance

2 hours · 90 marks · 33.3%

2 × 6-mark analyse
2 × 9-mark assess
1 × 15-mark evaluate
per case study

Two quantitative questions (9 or 15 marks)

Paper 2

Unit 3.2 — People and operations

2 hours · 90 marks · 33.3%

2 × 6-mark analyse
2 × 9-mark assess
1 × 15-mark evaluate
per case study

Two quantitative questions (9 or 15 marks)

Paper 3

Unit 3.3 — Society, environment and strategy

2 hours · 90 marks · 33.3%

2 × 6-mark analyse
2 × 9-mark assess
1 × 15-mark evaluate
per case study

One quantitative question + one drawing on data across the specification

Ethos
Virtue-led Business learning.
Business Studies at Nishkam is not only about passing exams. Every topic connects to who we are — a community learning to serve, to think carefully and to act with integrity.

Faith-Inspired

Learning from the wisdom of all traditions. Businesses that act with integrity and compassion reflect values at the heart of every great faith.

Virtues-Led

Fostering compassion, humility, justice and courage. Every business decision is an ethical choice — a chance to practise virtue or fall short of it.

Aspiring for Excellence

Becoming the best human beings we can possibly be. In Business Studies, this means thinking critically, writing with precision, and achieving the highest standards.

To empower children with virtues that enable them to excel academically and spiritually, inspiring them to serve humanity selflessly — with an abundance of love, compassion and forgiveness.

Nishkam School Trust — Mission Statement
Virtues
Peacefulness. Diligence. Determination.
Our three classroom virtues, lived inside the content of every lesson.

Peacefulness

Approach complex questions with a calm, focused mind. Listen to different stakeholder perspectives with respect. Create a learning environment where every voice is valued.

Diligence

Commit fully to mastering each topic. Check your chains of analysis, refine your evaluations, and never submit work that is less than your best.

Determination

Persevere through challenging exam questions. Treat every mistake as a stepping stone. Keep pushing toward your target grade and beyond.
