Specification
Three units of study.
Each unit has detailed notes, key definitions, worked examples, exam-style questions and links to the specification content.
Paper 1 · Unit 3.1

What is business? Managing marketing and finance

Entrepreneurs, business planning, objectives, forms of business, stakeholders, marketing management and financial management.

Entrepreneurs Objectives Marketing Finance
Paper 2 · Unit 3.2

Managing people and operations

HR objectives, organisational design, leadership, motivation, developing people, operations management and managing business culture.

HR planning Leadership Motivation Operations
Paper 3 · Unit 3.3 · A-level only

Business and society, external environment, and strategy

Corporate social responsibility, sustainability, ESG, the competitive and economic environment, strategic planning and managing change.

CSR & ESG External environment Strategy
Assessment
Three papers, same structure.
Every paper has two case studies. Each case study is followed by five compulsory questions worth 45 marks.

Paper 1

Unit 3.1 — Marketing and finance

2 hours · 90 marks · 33.3%

2 × 6-mark analyse
2 × 9-mark assess
1 × 15-mark evaluate
per case study

Two quantitative questions (9 or 15 marks)

Paper 2

Unit 3.2 — People and operations

2 hours · 90 marks · 33.3%

2 × 6-mark analyse
2 × 9-mark assess
1 × 15-mark evaluate
per case study

Two quantitative questions (9 or 15 marks)

Paper 3

Unit 3.3 — Society, environment and strategy

2 hours · 90 marks · 33.3%

2 × 6-mark analyse
2 × 9-mark assess
1 × 15-mark evaluate
per case study

One quantitative question + one drawing on data across the specification

Virtues
Peacefulness. Diligence. Determination.
Our three classroom virtues, lived inside the content of every lesson.

Peacefulness

Approach complex questions with a calm, focused mind. Listen to different stakeholder perspectives with respect. Create a learning environment where every voice is valued.

Diligence

Commit fully to mastering each topic. Check your chains of analysis, refine your evaluations, and never submit work that is less than your best.

Determination

Persevere through challenging exam questions. Treat every mistake as a stepping stone. Keep pushing toward your target grade and beyond.
